Old Road Tales Drafts

Old Road Tales Drafts is the channel's own desktop tool. Once a finished episode has been checked and approved, it sends the video to the channel's own accounts as a draft: a private upload on YouTube, and a draft in the TikTok inbox. The tool never posts anything publicly. Every video is looked over and posted by hand.

Only Old Road Tales uses it, for its own videos and its own accounts. It isn't offered to anyone else. How it handles sign-ins and data is set out in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
